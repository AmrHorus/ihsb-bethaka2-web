Perimeter

Perimeter is the length of the outer boundary of any geometric shape. It is used for geometric shapes such as squares, rectangles, circles, and triangles.



Rectangle Perimeter / Parallelogram Perimeter

The perimeter of a rectangle is the sum of its length and width multiplied by two, or length plus width.

2(L + W)

L + L + W + W

L = Length
W = Width



Square Perimeter / Rhombus Perimeter

The perimeter of a square is calculated by multiplying its side length by four or adding all four sides together.

4 × S

4S

S + S + S + S

S = Side

Circle Perimeter (Circumference)

The circumference of a circle is calculated by multiplying the diameter by pi, or multiplying twice the radius by pi.

D × π

2r × π

π ≈ 3.14
r = radius
D = diameter

Triangle Perimeter

The perimeter of a triangle is calculated by adding all three sides together.

a + b + c

a = First side
b = Second side
c = Third side



Trapezoid Perimeter

The perimeter of a trapezoid is calculated by adding all four sides together.

a + b + c + d

a = Longer base
b = Shorter base
c = Right side
d = Left side
